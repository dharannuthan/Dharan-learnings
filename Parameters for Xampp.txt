How to increase XAMPP perfomance parameters


C:\Users\dell\Desktop\xamp2\php\php.ini

memory_limit=512M (default)--> need to increase as per our ram size   -----2gb
post_max_size=40M (default)-->we can choose upto our max size 10 M to 50 M   ----------100 M
upload_max_filesize=40M (default)-->we can choose upto our max size 2 M to 50 M   ------100 M
